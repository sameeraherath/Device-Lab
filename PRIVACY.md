# Device Lab privacy policy

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

Device Lab is a free Chrome extension that shows the current web page inside device frames (phones, tablets, laptops and desktops) and lets you save screenshots of it.

## The short version

Device Lab does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data. Everything happens in Chrome on your own computer.

## What Device Lab does not do

- It has no account and no sign-up.
- It has no ads, analytics or tracking.
- It does not send any data to any server. Device Lab has no server.
- It does not read your browsing history.
- It does nothing until you click the Device Lab icon, and then only on that one tab.

## What is saved on your computer

Device Lab saves a few settings with Chrome's own storage, on your computer only, so they are still there next time:

- the last device you used and your recent devices
- the colour you chose for each device
- your screenshot options (mode, format and background)
- custom devices you added
- whether Focus mode was on

You can remove all of it at any time by removing the extension from Chrome.

## Screenshots

Screenshots are made on your computer. They are only saved where you choose, or copied to your clipboard when you click Copy. Device Lab never uploads them.

## Pages shown in the device frames

The page shown inside a device is the same page you are already on, loaded by Chrome in the usual way. To let sites that normally block frames appear inside the device, Device Lab removes that block only for the tab you clicked, only for the same site, and only while Device Lab is open there.

## Permissions

- **activeTab**: to work on the tab where you clicked the icon, and to take screenshots when you ask.
- **scripting**: to add the Device Lab panel to that tab.
- **storage**: to save the settings listed above on your computer.
- **declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess**: to let the current site show inside the device frames, as described above.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
